# Amplyst – User Guide
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Date:** 2025-11-25

## 1. Overview
Welcome to **Amplyst**! This platform is a digital marketplace designed to connect brands and agencies with top-tier influencers. This guide will walk you through using our platform to manage campaigns, apply for opportunities, and track performance.

**Key Features:**
*   **For Brands/Agencies**: Create detailed campaigns, review applications, and manage influencer relationships.
*   **For Influencers**: Browse a curated list of campaigns, apply with proposals, and submit content for review.
*   **Real-time Messaging**: Communicate directly within the platform.
*   **Secure Payments**: Manage payouts and campaign budgets transparently.

## 2. Quick Start
Getting started is simple. Here’s a quick rundown for each user type.

### 2.1 For Influencers
1.  **Sign Up**: Create an account and select "Influencer" as your role.
2.  **Complete Profile**: Fill in your niche, connect your social accounts, and add portfolio items during onboarding.
3.  **Browse Campaigns**: Visit your dashboard to see available campaigns that match your profile.
4.  **Apply**: Submit proposals to campaigns you love.
5.  **Collaborate**: Once accepted, use the messaging feature to coordinate with the brand and submit your content.

### 2.2 For Brands & Agencies
1.  **Sign Up**: Create an account and select "Brand" or "Agency".
2.  **Set Up Profile**: Add your company details and industry info.
3.  **Create Campaign**: Click "Create Campaign" on your dashboard. Define your budget, requirements, and target audience.
4.  **Review Applicants**: View influencer profiles who apply to your campaign. Approve the best fits.
5.  **Track Results**: Monitor campaign status and view analytics.

## 3. Guide to the Platform

### 3.1 Navigation
The main navigation bar provides links to:
*   **Dashboard**: Your central hub for all activity.
*   **Campaigns**: Browse or manage campaigns.
*   **Messages**: Access your chats.
*   **Profile/Settings**: Manage your account details.

### 3.2 Common Workflows

#### Creating a Campaign (Brands)
1.  Navigate to **Dashboard** > **Create Campaign**.
2.  Fill in the **Title**, **Description**, and **Budget**.
3.  Specify **Requirements** (e.g., "1 Instagram Reel").
4.  Publish the campaign. It is now visible to influencers.

#### Applying to a Campaign (Influencers)
1.  Go to **Campaigns** to view available opportunities.
2.  Click on a campaign to view details.
3.  Click **Apply**. You may need to write a short message explaining why you are a good fit.
4.  Monitor your **Applications** tab for status updates (Pending, Approved, Rejected).

#### Messaging
*   Click the **Messages** icon to open your inbox.
*   Chats are automatically created when an application is approved, allowing you to discuss details securely.

### 3.3 Mobile Experience
Amplyst is fully responsive. You can manage your campaigns, reply to messages, and check notifications from your smartphone or tablet.

## 4. Troubleshooting & FAQs

*   **"I can't see any campaigns."**
    *   Ensure your profile is complete. Some campaigns are targeted to specific niches or locations.
*   **"How do I get paid?"**
    *   Go to **Settings** > **Payments** to connect your payout method (e.g., Stripe). Payments are processed after campaign completion.
*   **"I'm stuck on the onboarding screen."**
    *   Make sure all required fields (marked with *) are filled out. If the issue persists, try refreshing the page.
